# Find a BA job after the internship

This page is for someone who **just finished** a Business Analyst internship and is applying to **Sri Lankan IT companies** (product, captive / GIC, and software services). Apply as a **Junior / Associate BA**, not as “still an intern” and not as a corporate strategy analyst.

If your intern company might still hire you, ask this week. Do not pause the rest of the search while you wait.

**Time:** 2–4 weeks of focused applications.  
**Rule:** Evidence over adjectives. User stories, a process map, and UAT notes beat “hardworking team player.”

## If the intern company might still hire

Ask your former manager and HR in writing (even if your last day already passed):

- Is there a junior / associate BA seat?
- Can they give a LinkedIn recommendation this week?
- Can they introduce you to another team or a client-side hiring manager?

A referral plus a recommendation still beats 80 cold applications. If the answer is no or slow, keep applying elsewhere the same day.

## Where to find roles

Use these, in this order:

1. **Former intern company** — referral to another squad if conversion is closed.
2. [LinkedIn Jobs](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/) — location Sri Lanka / Colombo. Alerts for the titles below.
3. [ITPro.lk](https://itpro.lk/) — Sri Lanka IT board.
4. [TopJobs](https://topjobs.lk/) — IT-Software and Corporate Management / Analysts.
5. **Company career pages** — IFS, Virtusa, Sysco LABS, LSEG Technology, WSO2, 99x, Cambio, DirectFN, CodeGen, Aeturnum, Mitra, Zone24x7, hSenid, Creative Software, GTN, BISTEC, Blue Lotus 360, NEXT Technology Lanka.
6. Batch WhatsApp, lecturers, and alumni — many Colombo junior IT hires still start as a referral.

Industry body: [SLASSCOM](https://slasscom.lk/) member directory and career fairs.

Skip intern.lk unless you are going back to studies. You are applying for a job, not another internship, unless a company only opens intern-to-hire pipelines.

## Titles to search

Junior Business Analyst, Associate Business Analyst, Business Analyst, Business Systems Analyst, Requirements Analyst, Product Analyst, junior Functional Consultant (ERP / product), Implementation Analyst, Associate Product Owner (only if the JD is stories + stakeholders).

Skip ads that require 3–5 years or CBAP unless they also say graduate / junior.

## Who hires junior BAs here

**Product / R&D:** IFS, WSO2, CodeGen, Cambio, DirectFN — fewer junior BA seats, stronger brand.

**Captive / GIC:** Sysco LABS, LSEG Technology, NEXT Technology, other UK/US/EU centres.

**Services / outsourcing:** Virtusa, 99x, Aeturnum, Mitra Innovation, Zone24x7, Creative Software, and many 20–200 person ISVs. Highest volume. Work is usually a US/UK/AU client: stories, UAT, English calls.

Pick **15 named companies**. Follow them on LinkedIn. Apply on the career page *and* LinkedIn when both exist.

## How the CV should look

**1 page PDF.** English. No photo. No icons. No two-column templates (ATS). Filename: `Firstname_Lastname_Junior_BA.pdf`.

**Target title on the CV is Junior Business Analyst** (or Associate BA). The internship is a *past job* with a start and end date — not “Present.”

What a Colombo IT hiring manager scans in 20 seconds:

- Completed internship in a **software** team
- Artefacts: user stories, process flows, UAT
- Tools: Jira, Confluence, Excel, maybe SQL and Figma comments
- Agile / Scrum used correctly
- English good enough for a client call

**Include:** city, phone, email, LinkedIn, 3–4 line summary, internship bullets with numbers (past tense), one sanitised piece of work, skills, education.

**Cut:** career objective, “Microsoft Office,” every club, duties with no outcome, “currently intern” if the internship ended.

Sinhala or Tamil: mention only if the firm has local clients. Export IT work is English.

Use the **formatted CV** on this site (Print → Save as PDF) or download the `.txt` files and paste into Word (Calibri 11, ~1.5 cm margins, one page). Replace every `[bracket]`.

## How to set up LinkedIn

Recruiters in Sri Lanka IT live here. Match the CV.

**Photo.** Head and shoulders, plain background, office or plain shirt.

**Custom URL.** `linkedin.com/in/yourname`.

**Headline.** Junior Business Analyst + skills + Colombo — not “BA Intern looking for opportunities.”

**Open to work.** Recruiters (public only if you want). Locations: Colombo / Sri Lanka. Titles: Junior Business Analyst, Associate BA, Business Systems Analyst.

**About.** Internship *completed* → what you did → tools → what you want next. First two lines must work before “see more.”

**Experience.** Same title and **end date** as the CV. Bullets, not a paragraph. Tag the company page.

**Featured.** Upload the 1-page CV PDF (no secrets) or a sanitised process-map image.

**Skills.** 12–15: Business Analysis, Requirements Gathering, User Stories, Acceptance Criteria, Jira, Agile, SQL, Microsoft Excel, Stakeholder Management, BPMN, UAT, Scrum. Ask your intern manager to endorse.

**Network.** Follow 25 target companies. Message 10 alumni with the note on this page.

**Job alerts.** `Junior Business Analyst Colombo`, `Associate BA Sri Lanka`.

## Interview pack

Typical Colombo IT process: HR English screen → BA/PM scenario round → sometimes a written case → manager.

Carry:

| Item | What “good” looks like |
| --- | --- |
| CV | 1-page PDF, internship as past experience |
| 5–8 user stories | Given/When/Then or a testable checklist |
| 1 process map | Swimlane As-Is / To-Be, including “they said no” |
| 1 internship case | Problem, stakeholders, artefacts, what shipped |
| UAT table | Scenario, steps, expected, status |
| Questions | Process, tools, how success is measured |

**Redact** client names, IDs, money. Write “UK retail client” / “internal HR module.”

### STAR stories (6)

1. Ambiguous requirement you clarified  
2. Two stakeholders wanted different things  
3. Everything was “urgent” — how you cut scope  
4. A process you mapped and what changed  
5. A missed requirement and how you recovered  
6. Working with a developer or QA who needed more from your AC  

### Questions they will ask

- Walk me through a requirement you wrote. How did developers use it?  
- Functional vs non-functional.  
- Write a user story and acceptance criteria on the board.  
- Client keeps changing scope — what do you do?  
- BA vs QA vs developer vs Scrum Master vs Product Owner.  
- Stakeholder cannot explain what they want.  
- Explain a sprint from a BA view.  
- “Login / leave request / checkout — write stories and a flow.”  
- Why did the internship end / why not convert? — honest, short, no blame. Then what you want next.

English: short, structured answers. Many juniors fail on communication, not on BABOK.

**Salary:** “open, aligned with junior BA in Colombo IT” until you have an offer. Ask a former intern or Glassdoor for a band; do not invent a number.

## Two-week plan

**Week 1**

- LinkedIn recommendation from intern manager  
- Rewrite CV from the template; Print → PDF; match LinkedIn dates  
- Sanitise 5 stories + 1 process map  
- Follow 25 companies; turn on alerts  
- Apply to 10 well-matched roles  

**Week 2**

- LinkedIn, ITPro.lk, TopJobs, and career pages — **8–10 quality applications / week**  
- Message 10 alumni  
- Two mocks: HR English screen + whiteboard stories  
- Tracker: company, role, date, link, next follow-up (7 days)  
- Follow up former company once if conversion was “maybe”

## What not to do

- Leave the CV titled “BA Intern” as the job you want  
- Leave internship dates as “Present” after the last day  
- Invent client metrics with AI  
- Send a 3-page CV  
- Apply only to Senior BA / CBAP ads  
- Post confidential intern artefacts on LinkedIn  
- Mass-message “Hi sir any vacancy?”
