# How to share this with your intern team

Send **one link**. That is the whole system.

After this site is published, copy the home URL and paste it into WhatsApp, email, Slack, or Teams. Interns open it on their phone or laptop. No login. No PDF pack to maintain. No Google Drive hunt.

If you are still in preview, the Preview link works for you. Interns outside this session cannot use it until you **Publish** the site (button in this Cursor chat). Then you get a normal public URL.

## Fastest way (2 minutes)

1. Publish this site so it has a public URL.
2. Send each intern the home URL. They pick Developer, QA, BA, or DevOps themselves.
3. Or send a role link: `/guides/developer`, `/guides/qa`, `/guides/ba`, `/guides/devops`.

A good message looks like this:

> Hi — open Intern Path and pick your role. Follow the weeks in order. Do the practice, not only the videos.
>
> [home URL]
>
> Everyone also reads the AI guide from the home page.
>
> If you are stuck more than 30 minutes, ask in standup with what you already tried.

## How interns should use a path

- **5–8 hours a week** is enough. Stretching it to 12 weeks is fine.
- Work **top to bottom**. The order is the point. AI makes it easy to skip around and stay shallow.
- Every week has **practice**. If they only open links, they are not ready.
- Checkboxes on the site stay on **that browser**. They are a personal notebook, not a gradebook.

## How you should coach (30 minutes a week)

Do not ask “did you finish the course?” Ask to **see the practice**:

- Developer: a GitHub repo or PR, a short demo, a bug they fixed.
- QA: a bug report, a test charter, a Postman collection.
- BA: a problem brief, stories with acceptance criteria, a process sketch.
- DevOps: a Dockerfile, a red-then-green pipeline log, a short runbook.

If they used AI, ask: “What did you verify yourself?” That one question builds industry habits.
